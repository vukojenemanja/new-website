The Inner Shift Method

Coaching and energy work
together as one.

After years of working with individuals and teams - Ideveloped this approach because I saw what conversation alone cannot do. The mind understands. The body and energy field are where it is truly held. With me, we work on both levels in every session.

01
We define the goal

Before we go anywhere, we get clear on where you actually want to end up. Not a vague wish - a real, felt sense of the life you are moving toward. That clarity becomes the direction for everything that follows.

02
We find the root of the blocks

Through conversation and meditative techniques, we go beneath the surface story to find where the pattern that is blocking you actually began - not just on a mental level, but where it is still held within you today.

03
We release it completely

Through energy work and techniques we release what we found - from the emotional, physical and energetic levels of your being.

04
We direct the free energy into real action

What is released becomes available to you. We use that energy to build clarity and the first real steps toward the life we defined in step one.

05
YOU become more YOU

Not a different person. More of who you truly are, beneath all that was blocking and protecting you. You feel lighter, clearer - and move more focused toward the life that has been waiting for you.
