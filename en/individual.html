Who this is for

You have tried.
But the feeling still hasn't changed.

You have read the books. You have worked on yourself. Maybe you have seen a therapist. And yet the same weight is there. The same fear. The same pattern that keeps repeating.

That is not a failure of will or understanding. The reason is that what holds us back does not live in the mind. It lives deeper - in the body, in the energy field, in places that thinking alone cannot reach.

  • There is a heaviness in your chest that follows you everywhere. You have learned to carry it, but it never really leaves.
  • You understand your patterns. You can name them, trace them, explain them. And yet here you are, living them again.
  • Fear stops you from moving forward - fear of failure, of judgment, of being truly seen. You know what you want. You cannot make yourself go there.
  • Life looks fine from the outside. On the inside something is hollow. You cannot explain it to anyone because on paper, everything is okay.
  • You feel emotionally exhausted. Not from doing too much - but from holding too much, for too long, alone.

None of that is who you are. It is what has been sitting on top of who you are, for a long time now.

This work is not about learning to manage the weight better. It is about putting it down - so you can find out what you actually want to do with the life underneath it.

Nemanja Vukoje

I was excellent at my job.
And I was slowly disappearing.

Years in corporate consulting. A career that made sense on paper. And then one day the feeling arrived - that my life was passing me by. I was stuck there for a while. But in the end I had to make a decision: will I respect myself and truly live my life, or will I just wait here and watch it pass?

I left. I traveled. I spent time with teachers in places I had never imagined I would go.

I wasn't just collecting certificates but experiences - what truly moves people, what truly heals, what it takes to make real change.

That experience defined and shaped this work.

What I found on that search is what I now offer you. Not a technique to manage your life better, but a way to truly live it - and to know, in your body, that you are not disappearing anymore.

The Inner Shift Method

Coaching and energy work
as one.

After years of working with individuals, teams and people in corporate environments - I developed this approach because I saw what conversation alone cannot do. The mind understands. The body and energy field are where it is truly held. With me, we work on both levels in every session.

01
We define the goal

Before we go anywhere, we get clear on where you actually want to end up. Not a vague wish. A real, felt sense of the life you are moving toward. That clarity becomes the compass for everything that follows.

02
We find the root of the blocks

Through conversation and meditative techniques, we go beneath the surface story to find where the pattern actually began. Not just on a mental level - but where it is still held within you today.

03
We release it completely

With energy healing, we release what was found - from the emotional, physical, and energetic levels of your being. Not by force. By finally giving it the space it has always been asking for.

04
We direct the free energy into real action

What releases does not just disappear - it becomes available to you. We use that energy to build clarity, momentum, and the first real steps toward the life we defined in step one.

05
You become more YOU

Not a different person. More of who you already were, beneath what was blocking you. Lighter, clearer and in motion - toward the life that has been waiting for you all along.

"I don't just help you understand what is holding you back. We release it from your system."
Why this combination works

Coaching shows you the root.
Energy healing removes it.

That is why this combination works where everything else only goes halfway.

With coaching alone, you understand your patterns - but they stay in the body. With energy healing alone, you feel better - but you do not know why or how to build on it.

Together, they create a complete process. We find exactly where the weight lives. Then we release it - not just from your mind, but from your body, your energy field, every level where it has been held. What was trapped comes back to you as energy, calm, and clarity.

That is the change people feel when they leave a session.
