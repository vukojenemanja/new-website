In Kundalini Yoga, your date of birth is read as a map. It produces five numbers, each describing a different part of your personality - your relationship with yourself, the lesson that repeats in relationships, a talent that comes easily, what others notice first, and the direction where you feel fulfilled.

For each number you find three things: your qualities, where your challenges lie, and what is worth focusing on.

The map gives you a language for what you already sense about yourself. Often one sentence is enough - you pause and think: yes, that is me.

No. The map is a tool for understanding yourself. The numbers show your qualities, challenges and themes - how you live them is up to you.
